## Analysis checklist before touching any title/description (FASE 12)

## Optimization rules
- **Title (FASE 13):** only with evidence; change must still accurately represent the page.
- **Meta description (FASE 14):** fix generic/duplicated/intent-mismatched — never stuff.
- **Testing (FASE 15):** one variable at a time; log BEFORE/AFTER in `experiments.md`;
  observe — don't declare success early (FASE 72: one change → measure → learn).
- **High CTR / low impressions (FASE 17):** signal of narrow demand or low exposure —
  not automatically "great" or "bad" content. Read it as one signal.
- **No premature optimization (FASE 61):** never change title/content/CTA/links just because
  a page had no immediate traffic — especially while the whole corpus is <30 days old.
