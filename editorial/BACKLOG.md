## Research notes (FASE 16 — SERP gap summaries, Sprint 5)

- **"what is a prenup"** — dominated by dictionaries (Cambridge, Merriam-Webster),
  Cornell Wex, ABA, and a 2022 Forbes piece. No focused, current, consumer-facing
  explainer with "what it covers / what it doesn't / how it works". Gap: plain-English
  depth + internal navigation.
- **"how long does a prenup take"** — results are brand blogs (First, Neptune) plus The
  Knot and small firms. Common structure: backward-from-wedding timeline + state timing
  rules. Gaps: inconsistent numbers (2–6 weeks vs 6–10 weeks), thin state-law handling
  (California's 7-day rule underexplained). Our angle: honest phase-by-phase timeline +
  state distinctions + link to the 30-days guide.
- **"do you need a lawyer for a prenup"** — Nolo (strong), Zola, HelloPrenup, law firms.
  Gaps: most bury the "depends on your state and situation" answer, few explain the
  half-representation problem plainly, online-review middle path is underexplored
  relative to its popularity. Our angle: decision framework + state examples + both
  commercial paths without pushing either.
- **"can you get a prenup after marriage"** — LegalShield, MetLife, Kiplinger, Nolo
  (2011!), Neptune. Several old or vendor-heavy pieces. Gap: a current, neutral explainer
  that also handles state variation (Iowa enforceability doubts, Minnesota counsel rule)
  without fear marketing.
