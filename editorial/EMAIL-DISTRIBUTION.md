# Email Distribution — The Prenup Brief (FASE 21–24)

Sending happens in MailerLite (external — no automation created in repo). This file is the editorial rulebook.

## FASE 21 — Selection criteria (all four, not one)
An article enters The Prenup Brief only if it is:
1. **genuinely useful** to an engaged couple, AND
2. **timely** (wedding horizon) or **important** (legal change, common trap) or **interesting** (fresh angle),
3. written to a *situation*, not to a sales goal,
4. able to stand alone as an email (the email must work even if nobody clicks).

Never auto-send every article.

## Verdicts for published articles
| Article | Brief? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| what-is-a-prenup | **YES** | Evergreen, zero commercial pressure, ideal first-read for new subscribers. |
| how-long-does-a-prenup-take | **YES** | Timely for anyone 3–6 months from the wedding; practical insight (7-day CA rule) worth the open. |
| do-you-need-a-lawyer-for-a-prenup | **HOLD** | High affiliate gravity; keep newsletter clean for now. Revisit after search data shows whether readers find it organically first. |

## FASE 22 — Format (one insight, one article, one next step)
```
Subject (curious, true, ≤50 chars)
Short intro — one sentence of lived context
One useful insight (the takeaway stands alone)
→ The article (single link)
Optional related resource (only if truly related)
Soft CTA (read/reply — not "buy")
```
Not a link list. One email = one idea.

## FASE 23 — Subjects (no clickbait; must match content)
Good: "Can a prenup protect a business?", "What actually affects the cost of a prenup?", "Three questions to ask before signing a prenup", "The 7 days California won't let you skip"
Bad: "You won't BELIEVE what happens without a prenup", anything implying guaranteed outcomes.

## FASE 24 — Reuse (adapt, don't copy)
One article → (a) newsletter email (editorial tone), (b) 1–3 social posts (channel-native tone), (c) short educational post / comment answer. Each format rewritten for its medium — never paste-forward.

## FASE 39 rhythm
At most one Brief feature per newly published article that passes the criteria above; not every sprint's output ships to email.
