# BRIEF-008 — Can a Prenup Protect Me From My Spouse's Debt?

**Status:** queued · **Priority:** P2

## PRIMARY TOPIC
Debt protection during marriage — how a prenup allocates existing and future debt, what state law does by default, and where the limits are (creditors aren't bound by the contract).

## PRIMARY SEARCH INTENT
Informational (anxiety-driven, protective).

**Why is this person searching this?** One partner carries meaningful debt (student loans, credit cards, a business line) and the other is quietly worried it becomes "their debt" — or vice versa.
