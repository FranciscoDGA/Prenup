# BRIEF-005 — What Does a Prenup NOT Cover?

## PRIMARY TOPIC
The limits of prenuptial agreements — what courts and state law won't let a prenup decide (child custody, child support, unenforceable terms) and what couples wrongly assume it handles.

## PRIMARY SEARCH INTENT
Informational.

**Why is this person searching this?** They already know what a prenup *is* and are now checking boundaries — either hoping it covers something it can't, or wanting reassurance about what it can't be used for.
