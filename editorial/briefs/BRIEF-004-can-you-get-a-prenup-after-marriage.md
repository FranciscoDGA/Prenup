## CORE QUESTION
"Can you get a prenup after you're already married?"

## SEARCH ANGLE
Timing / legal distinction: technically it's a *postnuptial* agreement, there's no deadline, but courts scrutinize postnups more closely (fiduciary duty) — and state rules range from Minnesota's mandatory counsel to Iowa's skepticism. Current, neutral, no fear marketing.

## RELEVANT STATE
Yes: all 50 states recognize postnups in some form, but requirements vary sharply — Minnesota: independent counsel mandatory; Ohio: statute (Ohio Rev. Code § 3103.06/§ 3103.061); New York: deed-level execution formality; Iowa: courts not required to enforce. Never generalize one state's rule.
