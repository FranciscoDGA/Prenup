## PRIMARY TOPIC
What prenups can and cannot do regarding children — courts decide custody and support based on the child's best interests; prenups decide property.

## PRIMARY SEARCH INTENT
Informational.

**Why is this person searching this?** Usually a parent in a second marriage trying to balance protecting their kids financially with what they've heard about "setting custody in the prenup".
