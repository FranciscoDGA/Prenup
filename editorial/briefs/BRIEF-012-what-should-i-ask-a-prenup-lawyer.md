## PRIMARY TOPIC
The exact questions to ask a prenup lawyer before and during the first consultation.

## PRIMARY SEARCH INTENT
- [x] Commercial-investigation (evaluating/prepare for a professional service)

**Why is this person searching this?** — They've decided a lawyer is probably involved and want to walk into the first meeting unprepared-panic-free (and not get upsold/blind-sided).

## USER SITUATION
researching

## TARGET READER
A 30–45-year-old American, usually with a house, business, or kids from a previous marriage, about to book (or just booked) a first consultation with a family-law attorney. Money-conscious, slightly intimidated, wants to know what a competent prenup lawyer looks like.

## CORE QUESTION
"What do I actually ask a prenup lawyer at the first meeting — and what answers tell me they're the right one?"

## SEARCH ANGLE
Questions **organized by decision purpose** (fees · process · disclosure · enforceability · disagreements · future changes · red flags) instead of a random top-10 — each group tied to a real decision the reader must make, with links to our cost guide (fee expectations) and state pages (state-specific asks). SERP is dominated by generic listicles (HelloPrenup, Attorney Inputs, firm blogs) — ours integrates with PrenupAnswers' decision content instead of floating alone.

## PRIMARY KEYWORD
"questions to ask a prenup lawyer" / "what to ask a prenuptial agreement attorney" — demand confirmed (HelloPrenup 10-questions, Attorney Inputs 2026, Neptune, FindLaw, multiple firm posts rank).

## SECONDARY QUESTIONS
1. How do prenup lawyers charge (flat vs hourly) and what should I expect?
2. What documents should I bring?
3. How do I know they have real prenup experience?
4. What happens if my partner and I disagree?
5. What makes an agreement unenforceable — and how do they prevent it?
6. Should my partner get their own lawyer?
