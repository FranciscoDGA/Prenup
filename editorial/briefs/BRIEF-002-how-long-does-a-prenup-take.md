## PRIMARY TOPIC
Realistic prenup timeline — how many weeks the process takes and how far before the wedding to start, working backward from the wedding date.

## PRIMARY SEARCH INTENT
Informational (practical planning).

**Why is this person searching this?** A wedding date exists on the calendar, one or both partners have agreed to get a prenup (or are about to), and they are asking: "is there still time, and when do we start?"

## USER SITUATION
getting-married

## TARGET READER
Engaged couple 1–8 months from the wedding, mid-planning, practical mindset. May be anxious about the 30-days-before question. Wants phases and numbers, not reassurance.

## CORE QUESTION
"How long does a prenup take, and how early do we need to start?"

## SEARCH ANGLE
Honest phase-by-phase timeline **backward from the wedding date**, with the state timing rules that actually constrain it (California's 7-day rule, the 30-day norm) — instead of vendor-branded "with our service it takes X weeks" claims.

## PRIMARY KEYWORD
"how long does a prenup take" — evidence: dedicated pages from First, Neptune, The Knot, and multiple firms (research note in BACKLOG.md).

Article (process/timeline)

## RELEVANT STATE
Yes: California's mandatory 7-day period between receiving the final draft and signing (Cal. Fam. Code § 1615); New Hampshire courts' 30-day guidance; most states set no fixed deadline — courts look at whether review time was adequate.
