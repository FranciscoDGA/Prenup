## FASE 52/53 — UTM + campaign naming
- **Internal links: never UTM'd.** UTM only on links *we publish externally* (social posts, newsletter buttons pointing to our own site — optional; external partner links keep partner attribution).
- `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` only when actually needed (e.g., `pinterest`, `linkedin`, `newsletter`, `reddit`).
- Campaign names (simple, lowercase-hyphen): `prenup-cost-guide`, `business-prenup`, `state-prenup-basics`, `what-is-a-prenup-launch`, `timeline-launch`.
