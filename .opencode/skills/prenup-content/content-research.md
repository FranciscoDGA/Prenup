# Content Research Engine — Stage 0b

Runs BEFORE the Content Director. Input: a USER TOPIC or an OPPORTUNITY block from the Content Opportunity Engine (`content-opportunity.md`). Answers: "What is actually happening around this search topic?" Produces a RESEARCH REPORT. Never writes article prose. Never copies competitor content — research only maps the editorial landscape.

## When web tools are available (websearch/webfetch)

Use them to: verify current facts, scan SERP, locate official sources, understand competition, find related questions, read public discussions. Never research just to look thorough — every search must answer a concrete editorial question.

## Investigation checklist

1. **Search intent** — primary + secondary intents. The keyword ≠ the intent (e.g. "prenup cost" can mean price, state cost, lawyer vs platform, who pays, alternatives). State what the person REALLY wants.
2. **SERP landscape** — for top results note: title, domain, content type, angle, apparent intent, strengths, weaknesses, missing info. No copying.
3. **Competing angles** — what law firms / legal sites / finance sites / relationship pubs are ALL saying… and what they are NOT saying.
4. **Content gaps** — the core deliverable. For each: Gap → Why it matters → Potential angle.
5. **Related questions** — classify HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW relevance. Only real found questions (or mark `[editorial hypothesis]`).
6. **Real reader language** — how ordinary people phrase the problem ("Can my prenup be thrown out?" vs "prenuptial agreement enforceability"). Gold for H2s, FAQs, headlines.
7. **Community insights** (Reddit/forums) — recurring questions, fears, misconceptions, objections. NEVER treat opinions as law ("People are worried about…" ≠ "The law says…").
8. **Authority sources** — for legal angles prioritize government/court/statute sources. Separate FACT / ANALYSIS / OPINION / ANECDOTE.
9. **Legal verification map** — General rule: […] · State-specific issues: […] · Claims requiring verification: […] · Recommended sources: […]. Never present state-variable law as universal.
10. **Angle discovery** — 3–5 angles, each with: Audience · Core promise · Search intent · Curiosity potential · Content gap · trade-offs. No artificial scores.
11. **Headline research** — one per category: SEO DIRECT · QUESTION · CURIOSITY · PROBLEM · SCENARIO · MISCONCEPTION.
12. **Internal content opportunities** — existing related articles from the SKILL.md catalog (potential link + why relevant) and missing supporting articles (potential future topic).
13. **Topic cluster** — pillar + cluster map; identify opportunities, do NOT auto-create them.
14. **Cannibalization check** — if PrenupAnswers already serves the same intent: EXISTING CONTENT OVERLAP (page, intent, overlap) → recommendation: UPDATE EXISTING / CREATE NEW / MERGE.
15. **Research confidence** — mark key conclusions HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW. Don't invent precision.

## Copyright

Never copy articles, paragraphs, lists, exclusive headlines, or comments. Research produces ORIGINAL content only.
