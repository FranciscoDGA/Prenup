# Editor — Stage 3 (final quality control)

Inputs: RESEARCH REPORT + CONTENT BRIEF (or CONTENT STRATEGY) + DRAFT. You do not rewrite blindly — you identify problems and correct only what is necessary.

## Checklist

1. **American English** — grammar, spelling, vocabulary, phrasing, idioms, sentence rhythm. Everything natural to an American reader.
2. **AI/artificial writing** — repetitive structures, generic introductions, unnecessary transitions, predictable paragraphs, overused "important to note", robotic conclusions, unnatural vocabulary. Rewrite where found.
3. **SEO** — H1, H2/H3 hierarchy, primary keyword present but unstuffed, search intent satisfied, meta title/description, URL slug, FAQ, internal links.
4. **Reader value** — does it actually answer the core question? Useful context? No filler?
5. **Trust** — unsupported claims? Legal claims consistent with the Legal Verification section of the Research Report? Nothing invented. List verified vs requiring-verification claims.
6. **Intent consistency** — the angle/hook in the brief still delivered in the draft?
7. **Conversion** — CTA follows naturally? No aggressive sales language? Affiliate links only where the brief allowed?
8. **Internal links** — all targets exist in the SKILL.md catalog? No invented pages?
