Two platforms. Same target couple. And — this is the part that surprises people — **the same effective base price.**

First and HelloPrenup both sell guided, state-specific prenups built for regular couples, not billionaires. What separates them hides in the second half of the pricing page: what happens when lawyers get involved, how notarization is handled, and which one your state situation prefers.

## The head-to-head

| | **First** | **HelloPrenup** |
|---|---|---|
| Base package | **$649** Self-Serve (*notarization included*) | **$599** + $50 e-notarization = **$649** |
| Attorney tier | **$3,500** Lawyer Review (attorney for each partner, notarization included) | **~$2,000** per couple ($699–$799 per partner, notarization included) |
| Quick legal question | Package-based | **$49** per 20-minute attorney Q&A |
| State coverage | State-specific at checkout | 49 states published |
| Payment plans | Package pricing | Afterpay / Klarna available |
| Model | One guided flow, two tiers | À la carte add-ons around a $599 core |
| Notarization | Included from the start | $50 add-on (free with attorney tier) |

**Read the first two rows together:** the base tiers are a dead heat at $649. The attorney tiers are not even close.

## Round 1: The self-serve tier → draw

$649 with notarization (First) versus $599 + $50 notarization (HelloPrenup). You're choosing interfaces, not prices. Both give you:

- A guided questionnaire you complete **as a couple**, each with your own login
- State-specific documents with disclosure schedules built in
- Electronic signing with online notarization options
- Flat fee — no hourly meter, no retainer

Pick whichever flow feels better in the first ten minutes. That's a legitimate tiebreaker when price and substance match.

## Round 2: With attorneys → HelloPrenup, by $1,500

This is where the comparison stops being close:

- **First:** Lawyer Review = **$3,500 flat** per couple, notarization included. Clean, premium, done-for-you.
- **HelloPrenup:** Attorney representation ≈ **$699–$799 per partner → ~$2,000** per couple, notarization included, plus a network of 100+ attorneys across 49 states — and a $49 quick-question option for couples who only need an hour of guidance.

Identical legal outcome — a licensed attorney reviews, customizes, and signs as counsel of record — at roughly **40% less** with HelloPrenup. Unless First's included polish is worth $1,500 to you, the math is the math.

## Round 3: The state-specific edge

Here's a detail most comparisons miss, and it matters if you're in one of these states:

HelloPrenup's attorney package is the **required path** where state law demands representation — including spousal-support waivers in **California, Alabama, New York, and Washington**, and enforceable agreements in **South Carolina and West Virginia**. If you live there and want support terms, the "just the $599 tier" option isn't available to you regardless of platform — so a service that prices the attorney route at ~$2,000 instead of $3,500 earns its keep.

(Not sure what your state demands? The [cost calculator](/tools/prenup-cost-calculator/) shows your local rules and price bands.)

## Round 4: Cash flow during wedding season

Wedding season is a cash vacuum: venue, rings, flights, dresses. HelloPrenup's Afterpay/Klarna checkout splits the $599 into interest-free installments — First presents flat package pricing. Neither is wrong; if spreading the cost over months keeps the prenup from slipping to "after the honeymoon" (where it becomes a much harder postnup), that's a real-world advantage.

## Who should pick which

**Pick [First](https://www.thisfirst.com/?via=francisco) if:**
- You want one simple choice: self-serve or full lawyer package, nothing in between
- Included notarization in the base price appeals and you'd rather never think about add-ons
- You prefer its guided flow after trying both (both offer free starts — take ten minutes in each)

**Pick [HelloPrenup](https://helloprenup.com/?via=francisco) if:**
- You want the attorney tier — you'll save roughly $1,500 for the same protection
- You're in a state requiring representation for your clauses (CA/AL/NY/WA waivers, SC/WV)
- You might only need a **$49 quick lawyer question** instead of a full review
- Spreading payment over installments matters right now
- You value the deeper prenup-only specialization and 100+ attorney network

**The honest verdict:** at $649, you can't lose on either. The decision only matters when lawyers enter the picture — and then HelloPrenup's pricing wins for almost every couple that isn't shopping purely for maximum bundled simplicity.
