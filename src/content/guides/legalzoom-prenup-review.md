LegalZoom is the name your parents trust for "legal stuff online." Twenty years, millions of documents, a brand that means *legitimate* to people who've never hired a lawyer.

So you search "prenup LegalZoom," land on their page, and — if you look closely — discover something interesting: **LegalZoom doesn't really sell a prenup anymore. It sells you to someone who does.**

That's not an insult. It's the single most important fact in this review, because it tells you exactly what you're buying — and where you should probably click instead.

## What LegalZoom's prenup actually is

LegalZoom's prenup offering has two halves:

### 1. The exclusive HelloPrenup partnership (the main path)

LegalZoom's official prenup page routes couples into **HelloPrenup's** guided platform — exclusive partnership, same $599 flat fee per couple, same questionnaire, same state-specific document. LegalZoom's page effectively becomes a storefront: brand trust on the front, HelloPrenup's product behind it.

**What this means for you:** the product is identical to buying HelloPrenup direct. Same price. The differences live in what surrounds the purchase — and what you *don't* get by arriving through the storefront (payment plans, à la carte add-ons, the direct relationship).

### 2. The attorney-assisted tier (the real differentiator)

Through its law firm, LegalZoom offers a **flat-fee attorney-assisted prenup — roughly $1,499 per couple with notarization included** — in about 12 states: California, Arizona, Texas, Florida, Pennsylvania, New York, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, Tennessee, Colorado, and Ohio (expanding; confirm yours at checkout).

A licensed co-counsel attorney is involved from the start — not reviewing a finished draft, but shaping it. That's a meaningfully different product from both the $599 self-serve tier and the $3,000+ hourly-lawyer world.

## What LegalZoom does well

- **Attorney-first without hourly billing.** $1,499 flat with a real co-counsel and notarization included sits below HelloPrenup's attorney tier and miles below traditional representation. For a moderately complex situation in a supported state, that's the sweet spot in this entire industry.
- **Brand gravity and accountability.** BBB-scale recognition, twenty years of operation, one throat to choke. For risk-averse couples, that psychological comfort is worth real money.
- **Convenience for the already-customers.** If your LLC, your will, and your trademarks live in LegalZoom, keeping the prenup in the same dashboard is legitimate value.

## Where it falls short

- **The storefront tax is zero — which is the point.** Buying the $599 HelloPrenup product *through* LegalZoom costs the same as going direct. So the only reason to route through them is brand comfort; the reason **not** to: you skip HelloPrenup's direct checkout benefits (Afterpay/Klarna installments, the full à-la-carte menu, direct support).
- **12-state ceiling on the good tier.** The attorney-assisted offer is genuinely attractive — and unavailable to most of the country. If your state isn't on the list, LegalZoom's remaining value proposition is just HelloPrenup's product with fewer frills.
- **Prenups are one shelf of a giant store.** HelloPrenup's entire company is prenups; LegalZoom's is not. The specialization shows in depth of questionnaire, attorney network sizing, and prenup-specific support.
- **Complexity ceiling still applies.** The attorney tier helps with moderate complexity — but a business valuation or multi-state property puzzle wants a family-law specialist who does *only* this, not a network co-counsel on a flat fee.

## The verdict, by who you are

**You already live in LegalZoom + you're in a supported state + you want a lawyer from the start →** the $1,499 tier is a fair offer. Compare it against HelloPrenup's ~$2,000 representation and local attorneys' hourly quotes, then decide — none of those three answers is wrong.

**You're price-sensitive, or outside the 12 states, or want installments →** skip the storefront. The product LegalZoom would hand you is HelloPrenup's — <a href="https://helloprenup.com/?via=francisco" rel="sponsored noopener">start there directly</a> and keep the full add-on menu and payment plans.

**Business, multiple properties, trusts, big inequality →** none of the above. Hire a family-law attorney ([here's how the lawyer-vs-online line falls](/guides/lawyer-vs-online-prenup/)), and treat platforms as the drafting layer only.
